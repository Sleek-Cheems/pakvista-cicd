Badshahi Mosque

The Badshahi Mosque is one of Lahore's most famous landmarks. Built by Mughal Emperor Aurangzeb in 1673, it is known for its grand architecture and historical significance.

Lahore Fort

Lahore Fort is a UNESCO World Heritage Site that reflects the rich Mughal history of Pakistan. The fort contains beautiful palaces, halls, and gardens visited by thousands of tourists.

Shalimar Gardens

The Shalimar Gardens are among the finest Mughal gardens in South Asia. Their fountains, terraces, and greenery attract visitors from around the world.
