Hunza Valley

The Hunza Valley is a mountainous valley in the northern part of the Gilgit-Baltistan region of Pakistan, formed by the Hunza River. It is famous for its stunning natural beauty, high alpine peaks, and rich cultural heritage.

Rakaposhi Viewpoint

Rakaposhi is a mountain in the Karakoram range. The viewpoint offers an unobstructed, majestic view of the Rakaposhi peak, making it one of the most popular stops for tourists traveling along the Karakoram Highway.

Attabad Lake

Attabad Lake was formed in 2010 due to a massive landslide. Today, it is famous for its strikingly vibrant turquoise blue waters, offering activities like boating, jet skiing, and fishing amidst the surrounding mountains.

Baltit Fort

Baltit Fort is an ancient fort in the Hunza Valley that served as the residence of the Mirs of Hunza. With its Tibetan-influenced architecture, the fort has been beautifully restored and now serves as a museum, offering a glimpse into the region's history.
