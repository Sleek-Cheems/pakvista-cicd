About Karachi

Karachi is the largest city of Pakistan and serves as the country's economic and financial center. It is known for its beaches, historical buildings, museums, and vibrant culture.

Clifton Beach

Clifton Beach is one of Karachi's most popular attractions. Visitors enjoy horse riding, camel riding, seaside walks, and beautiful sunset views along the Arabian Sea.

Mohatta Palace

Mohatta Palace is a historic building constructed in 1927. It now serves as a museum that showcases Pakistan's cultural heritage, architecture, and historical exhibitions.

Pakistan Maritime Museum

The Pakistan Maritime Museum highlights the country's naval history. Visitors can explore exhibits, naval equipment, and a retired submarine displayed on the museum grounds.
